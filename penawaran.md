## 1. RINGKASAN SISTEM

**Jastip ERP Retail & Titip-Beli** adalah sistem manajemen bisnis terintegrasi yang menggabungkan:

| Fitur Utama | Keterangan |
|---|---|
| 🛒 **E-commerce** | Katalog produk, keranjang, checkout, pembayaran multi-metode |
| 💰 **Sistem Poin** | 1 poin = Rp 1, top-up, tabungan, reward, kadaluarsa |
| 🏪 **Multi-Hub** | Manajemen cabang distribusi di berbagai kota |
| 📦 **Subscription** | Langganan sembako berkala dengan auto-renewal |
| 🏭 **ERP Back-office** | Warehouse (WMS), stok, pengiriman, keuangan, RAB |
| 🔐 **5 Role Pengguna** | Guest, Customer, Hub Owner, Superadmin, Admin/Operator (khusus per modul) |
| 📊 **Skala Sistem** | 100+ halaman, 106 tabel database, modul WMS & Procurement |

### 1.1 Role Pengguna

| Role | Deskripsi |
|---|---|
| **Guest** | Pengunjung belum login — akses landing, katalog, login, register |
| **Customer** | Pembeli end-user — belanja, poin, reward, subscription, komplain |
| **Hub Owner** | Pemilik cabang — kelola stok, pengiriman, laporan fee |
| **Superadmin** | Admin pusat — akses penuh semua modul, keuangan, master data |
| **Admin/Operator** | Khusus per modul (keuangan, gudang, CS, dll) — akses terbatas sesuai penugasan |

### 1.2 Modul Utama Sistem

| No | Modul | Deskripsi |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Autentikasi & Manajemen User | Login, register, 5 role, permission, audit trail |
| 2 | E-commerce | Katalog, detail produk, keranjang, checkout, riwayat pesanan |
| 3 | Sistem Poin & Wallet | Top-up, transaksi, tabungan, kadaluarsa poin |
| 4 | Membership & Tier | Bronze–Diamond, upgrade otomatis, benefit |
| 5 | Reward & Loyalty | Klaim reward, event, referral |
| 6 | Subscription | Paket langganan, auto-renewal, siklus tagihan |
| 7 | Multi-Hub & Distribusi | Manajemen cabang, penugasan customer per radius |
| 8 | Warehouse & Stok (WMS) | Lokasi/bin, stok, opname, mutasi, replenishment |
| 9 | Pengiriman & Tracking | Delivery, kurir, tracking 7 status, retur |
| 10 | Procurement | Order stok, PR/PO, supplier, vendor scoring, backorder |
| 11 | Keuangan & Akuntansi | COA, jurnal, RAB, fee split, laporan |
| 12 | Komplain & CS | Tiket, chat, SLA, eskalasi |
| 13 | Notifikasi | Email, WhatsApp, in-app |
| 14 | Dashboard & Laporan | Dashboard per role, export Excel/PDF |
